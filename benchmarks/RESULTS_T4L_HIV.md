### T4 — null test of `conformatlas compare` (all 10 splits of the 5 ff99SB*-ILDN replicates into 2 + 3)

**FAIL: bootstrap false-positive rate 1.00** over 20 (split, state) pairs (gate ≤ 0.15; nominal 0.05). Welch test on per-replicate populations: false-positive rate 0.00 (Welch p 0.33–0.57).

Why: the replicates do not sample the same distribution. In the shared space one replicate (md1us4) is mostly in a second state while the other four are mostly in the first, so whichever group holds md1us4 differs by 24–52 percentage points. The block bootstrap resamples frames *within* replicates; its median 95 % half-width is 9.9 pp, while the median replicate-to-replicate SD is 30.4 pp. The intervals therefore describe uncertainty given these particular trajectories, not the variability between independent simulations, and they are far too narrow for a between-condition claim. Per-split values: `t4l/results/t4_null_test_table.csv`.
