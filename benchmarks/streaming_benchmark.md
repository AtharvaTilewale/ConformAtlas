Notes

- The streaming path reads every trajectory twice (moments, then projections); its wall time is therefore bounded below by two passes of XTC decoding + Kabsch superposition, which dominates for small systems.
- Memory of the streaming path is independent of the number of frames: one `D × D` float64 matrix (1233² × 8 B ≈ 12 MB here) plus one chunk; the in-memory path grows linearly with frames (float32 coordinates plus float64 working copies inside the PCA).
- A leading-eigenvalue difference of order 1e-15 confirms that both paths compute the same covariance; the full validation (eigenvalues, eigenvectors, projections, both weightings) is in `validation/checks_performance.py`.

## Summary

- At 81000 frames the in-memory path peaks at 2626 MB and the streaming path at 227 MB (11.6× less); streaming wall time is 3.7× the in-memory time (33.5 s vs 9.1 s).
- Four replicates (81000 frames): streaming, 4 workers take 18.1 s vs 27.9 s with one worker (1.55× speed-up); the remaining time is the serial eigendecomposition, result transfer and XTC decoding.
